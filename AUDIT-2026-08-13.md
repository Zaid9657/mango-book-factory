## Key findings

1. **No launch was ever run** — zero promo, ads, email, outreach. Book 1 is an
   *unrun* test, not a failed one. KU's zero borrows prove zero visibility.
2. **Series cross-sell broken** — Amazon shows "Book 1 of 1"; Book 2's title
   field lacks the series number.
3. **Listings can't convert**: no interior images in Look Inside, no A+
   Content, no author name on covers or title pages, text-only back cover,
   24 pages at $12.99 against $6–12 hardcovers, reflowable (not fixed-layout)
   Kindle files, wrong categories (no ADHD/Special-Needs node), wrong age band
   (6–9; the market for this format is 4–8).
4. **Niche already occupied** by 4+ operators running the same playbook
   (Kirschner-Maleikha, Robin Whimsy, Hello ADHD Brain, Vivi Lark) with
   review head starts. Mango absent from top 16 for its own core query.
5. **Playbook defects**: Day-30 gate measures Kindle BSR (conflates $0.05 KU
   borrows with sales, ignores print where ~100% of revenue is); free-ebook
   newsletter promos are an adult-fiction mechanic that converts ~0 for
   picture books; "3–5 reviews from Zaid's network" is Amazon review abuse —
   account-level risk; BSR thresholds cargo-culted with no rule for the
   50k–300k range where reality lands.
6. **Compliance risks**: KDP AI-content disclosure must be verified on all
   three titles (highest expected-cost item — account termination would also
   hit MedMeister-adjacent infrastructure); credential claim inconsistent
   ("by" vs "with the guidance of" a child psychiatrist); AI illustrations
   likely uncopyrightable in the US (no enforceable character IP).
7. **The real constraint is demand, not supply.** The pipeline solved
   production; nothing in the plan creates a demand channel. MedMeister's
   German-physician audience is an unused distribution asset.

## Verdict

**FIX — narrowly, time-boxed to ~12 hours, KILL as the base case. The
13-book plan is dead now.** Books 3–13 would cost 50–100 hours and buy zero
information. Fix the two live listings, point one real traffic source at
them, let the market answer once.
