# Mango the Crocodile — 13-Book Series Plan

One feeling/skill per book. Each entry: topic — back-ridge color — Gogo's sticky line — calm-down skill taught.

1. ✅ **The Busy Brain** (attention/ADHD) — yellow→gray→green — "Don't trap it — aim it." — aiming attention, the return is the skill. SHIPPED.
2. ✅ **The Worry Cloud** (anxiety) — yellow — "A worry shrinks when you say it out loud." — name the worry + 3 slow breaths. SHIPPED.
3. **The Red-Hot Back** (anger) — red-orange — "Anger is a wave. You don't fight a wave — you let it pass." — pause, breathe like blowing out candles, then speak.
4. **The Heavy Rain Day** (sadness) — blue (introduce blue to chart) — "Sad needs company, not fixing." — it's okay to feel it; find your Gogo.
5. **The Too-Big Spark** (overexcitement/impulse) — bright pink (introduce) — "A spark is great — point it before it jumps." — stop-think-go.
6. **The Knot in the Tail** (frustration / wanting to quit) — red-orange flicker — "Stuck isn't stopped. Try a smaller bite." — break it into tiny steps.
7. **The First Splash** (fear of new things / first day) — yellow — "Brave isn't unafraid. Brave is one small splash." — one tiny step counts as courage.
8. **The Losing Game** (losing / being a good sport) — gray — "A game ends. A friend doesn't." — feelings after losing are normal; shake, breathe, say good game.
9. **The Wobbly Tower** (mistakes / perfectionism) — yellow→gray — "Wobbly towers teach. Perfect ones don't." — mistakes are information.
10. **The Green-Eyed Ripple** (jealousy) — introduce envy-green shade — "Wanting what they have hides what you have." — name it, then count your own.
11. **The Little Voice Inside** (self-talk) — gray→green — "Talk to yourself like you'd talk to a friend." — catch the mean voice, swap the sentence.
12. **The Quiet Roar** (shyness / speaking up) — yellow — "Your voice fits you. Use your size of loud." — practice one sentence, say it once.
13. **The Long Dark Quiet** (bedtime worries) — yellow→soft green — "Night thoughts are louder, not truer." — worry parking: tell it 'tomorrow', then one calm picture.

## Rules
- Never reorder without Zaid's say-so; default = next unshipped number.
- Each book introduces at most ONE new chart color.
- Parent note must address THIS book's feeling specifically, not generic SEL.
- Titles stay in the "The ___" pattern after book 1; series brand is "Mango the Crocodile".
