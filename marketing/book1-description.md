Does your child's brain race ahead of the classroom — and "just concentrate"
only makes it worse?

Mango the little crocodile knows the feeling. His thoughts move faster than the
lesson, in every direction at once, and the harder he tries to trap them the
louder they get. Then Gogo the old turtle gives him a different idea: don't trap
it — aim it.

Why parents love this book:

★ Teaches a real technique children can use — attention isn't something you cage,
it's something you point, lose, and point again

★ The colour-changing bumps on Mango's back give children words for what they
feel before they can explain it

★ A failed first attempt shows kids that not getting it right away is normal

★ Includes a practical Note for Parents — what a busy brain actually is, why the
instinctive response backfires, and four strategies that work at home

★ Written by a child & adolescent psychiatrist — clinically grounded, never
preachy

From the Mango the Crocodile series. Perfect for ages 4–8, reading together or
alone.
